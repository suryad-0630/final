Distance makes small things feel big.

When you live in another city, a missed insurance renewal or an unbooked check-up turns into stress, extra cost and guilt. Calling to chase it isn't a plan.

  • Did Dad renew the car insurance?
  • Is Mom's health check-up booked?
  • Who can fix the water heater this week?
  • Are her documents about to expire?

How LifeDesk works for your family

You stay in charge from wherever you are. We handle the doing.

  1. You tell us what's neededBy app, website, WhatsApp or call. For example an insurance renewal or a service booking.
  2. We verify the detailsTiming, location and the right type of provider for your parent.
  3. A coordinator takes overThey work with verified service partners in your parent's city.
  4. We book and follow upSchedule, confirm and chase until it's done.
  5. You get confirmationWe confirm completion and ask for your feedback.
